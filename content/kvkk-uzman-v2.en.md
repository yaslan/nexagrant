# Expert Pre-registration Privacy Notice

Version: kvkk-v2

This notice is given under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") to people who fill in the FonBridge Expert Directory pre-registration form. The Turkish text is the governing version.

## 1. Data controller

Fonbridge Eğitim, Proje ve Danışmanlık Ltd. Şti. ("FonBridge"). Contact: info@fonbridge.com

## 2. Personal data processed

- Identity and contact: full name, e-mail address, province.
- Professional details: title/organisation, freelance status, areas of expertise, project roles, programme experience, project codes, ORCID, LinkedIn address, working languages.
- Registration details: who referred you, campaign source (UTM), consent timestamps.
- Security: an irreversible digest (hash) of your IP address. Your IP address itself is not stored.

## 3. Purposes

- To receive your pre-registration and verify your e-mail address.
- To assign a Founding Expert number and build the expert directory at launch.
- To prevent abuse and automated (bot) registrations.
- If you opted in, to send you FonBridge announcements by e-mail.

## 4. Legal bases

- Necessary for entering into or performing a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c): pre-registration and verification.
- Legitimate interest of the controller (KVKK Art. 5/2-f): abuse prevention.
- Explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1): publishing your profile in the directory and announcement e-mails.

## 5. Transfers

Your data is not transferred abroad. It is processed in Turkey and shared only with the Turkish service providers that host the service and send e-mail, and only for that purpose. Your profile becomes visible to directory users only if you consented to its publication in the directory and only after the directory launches. Until launch, your profile is not shown to anyone. Your e-mail address is never shown publicly in the directory.

## 6. Retention

Your pre-registration data is kept for 1 (one) year from the date you first submitted the form and is then deleted. The same period applies to unverified registrations. You may ask for deletion before this period ends.

## 7. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you may learn whether your data is processed, request information, ask for correction or deletion, withdraw consent and object. You can withdraw your consent to announcement e-mails and to directory publication by writing to info@fonbridge.com. Send any other requests to the same address.
